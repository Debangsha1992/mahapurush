# Two Mode Content Model

MindSpark will keep only two user-facing learning modes: Facts Mode and Introspection.

Facts Mode is the lightweight discovery experience. It presents short, source-backed, bite-sized Approved Facts about NotablePeople, with each Fact focused on a memorable detail or mini-story rather than rankings, popularity metrics, or long biographies. A published NotablePerson may have zero Approved Facts until research and editorial review are complete.

Introspection is the deeper interactive learning journey. It can use stories, struggles, contributions, teachings, and assessment-style prompts to help users understand how notable people thought and what learners can apply from their lives.

This preserves a clear product boundary: quick curiosity belongs in Facts Mode, while deeper reflection belongs in Introspection. It also prevents the content overhaul from turning Facts Mode into a lesson surface, creating additional competing modes, or filling the feed with generated placeholder Facts just to satisfy a fixed count.
