# MindSpark Learning Context

MindSpark presents source-backed learning moments about people who shaped human thought, culture, public life, and discovery.

## Language

**People Library**:
The first-class browse and search experience for curated NotablePerson records.
_Avoid_: Facts Mode, Thinker Library, database

**NotablePerson**:
A lightweight curated profile for a historical or contemporary person whose contribution is worth surfacing in the People Library, Facts Mode, and optionally Introspection.
_Avoid_: great person, hero, full thinker

**Featured NotablePerson**:
A NotablePerson selected for prominent People Library placement and ordered by featured rank.
_Avoid_: favorite, hero, full thinker

**Thinker**:
A curated learning profile with lessons, a long life story, and app journey content.
_Avoid_: notable person, profile

**Fact**:
A short, source-backed, bite-sized learning card about one NotablePerson. A Fact should surface a specific memorable detail or mini-story, not a ranking metric or long biography.
_Avoid_: trivia, biography, endorsement, ranking metric, lesson

**Draft Fact**:
A candidate Fact or source note that is not visible to users. Draft Facts may be generated or awaiting review, but they are not Facts Mode content.
_Avoid_: approved fact, published fact, user-facing fact

**Approved Fact**:
A Fact that has passed source and editorial review and is allowed to appear in Facts Mode and public profile surfaces.
_Avoid_: draft fact, generated fact, placeholder

**Facts Mode**:
The lightweight discovery experience where users browse short, source-backed Approved Facts about NotablePeople. Facts Mode is not the place for long-form lessons, assessments, draft facts, or deep reflective journeys.
_Avoid_: People Library, quiz, lesson, Introspection

**Introspection**:
The in-depth interactive learning journey where users reflect on what they can learn from highlighted people, including their stories, struggles, contributions, and ways of thinking.
_Avoid_: Facts Mode, onboarding, trivia
