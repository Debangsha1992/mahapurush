# MindSpark Learning Context

MindSpark presents source-backed learning moments about people who shaped human thought, culture, public life, and discovery.

## Language

**NotablePerson**:
A lightweight profile for a historical or contemporary person whose contribution is worth surfacing in Facts mode.
_Avoid_: great person, hero, full thinker

**Thinker**:
A curated learning profile with lessons, a long life story, and app journey content.
_Avoid_: notable person, profile

**Fact**:
A short, source-backed learning card about one NotablePerson.
_Avoid_: trivia, biography, endorsement

**Facts Mode**:
The lightweight discovery experience where users browse one source-backed Fact at a time.
_Avoid_: quiz, lesson

**Introspection**:
The personal learning journey where users reflect on what they can learn from highlighted people.
_Avoid_: quiz, onboarding
